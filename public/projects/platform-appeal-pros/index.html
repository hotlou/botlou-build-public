the story

Platform Appeal Pros is a consulting business with a very specific premise. Instagram's automated enforcement has gotten aggressive enough that real creators, real small businesses, and real everyday users are getting disabled without warning, often for things that are not actually violations. The official appeal process is slow, confusing, and frequently incorrect. The business exists to bring structure and professionalism to a situation where most people have no idea what to do.

The work is documentation and strategy, not magic. A case evaluation identifies the violation type, account history, and prior appeal attempts. Evidence is prepared and formatted in a way that aligns with Meta's actual review policies. The escalation pathway is chosen based on pattern recognition across thousands of prior cases. There is no impersonation, no backdoors, no pretending to be connected to Meta — the firm is explicitly independent and discloses that on every page.

The service tiers are honest about their own success ranges, which is unusual in this category. A $15 self-guided kit is labeled at 1–3% historical success because self-guided appeals rarely work against aggressive enforcement. A $995 full-service engagement with a real case audit, real evidence compilation, and real escalation support lands in the 70–92% range. A $1,995 priority tier with a dedicated specialist pushes that higher. The 110% money-back guarantee covers service delivery, not outcomes — the firm guarantees the work, not platform behavior.
