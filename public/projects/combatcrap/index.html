the story

combatcrap.com is Lucy Berman's personal home on the internet. She is a registered dietitian and an amateur MMA fighter — an unusually credible combination in the supplement space — and the co-founder of Combat Candy. The site exists because "the Combat Candy cofounder who is actually an athlete and actually a dietitian" is a real differentiator, and that story deserved a proper home outside the storefront.

The site pulls double duty. It is a personal brand hub for Lucy — credentials, fight and training background, content — and it is also where the creatine education content lives that supports Combat Candy's SEO. Keeping that content on combatcrap.com instead of inside the store is intentional: personal authority lives on a personal site, and the product site gets to stay focused on conversion.
